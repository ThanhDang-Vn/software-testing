# Usability Evaluation Objective

## Research objective

Determine whether typical Vietnamese online shoppers can sign in, independently
discover their personal order history, and correctly interpret the latest
order's ID, date, total, and current status without moderator guidance.

## Research questions

1. Can participants recover from ordinary login input errors?
2. Can participants discover Order History after successful authentication?
3. Can they identify the newest order and accurately report all four FR-11
   fields?
4. Are translated status labels and color treatments understandable?
5. Do feedback and navigation inspire confidence and trust?

## Target user profile

- Age 18 or older.
- Has used an e-commerce website or mobile app at least once.
- Not currently enrolled in this HW03 class.
- Preferably non-IT and not a professional software tester.
- Can read Vietnamese and voluntarily consents to participation.

## Success measures

- Completion: all requested information reported correctly.
- Unassisted completion rate.
- Completion time from scenario handoff to final answer.
- Errors, wrong turns, hesitations ≥5 seconds, and interventions.
- SUS score per participant and overall mean.
