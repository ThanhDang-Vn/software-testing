### QA/QC Job Market Summary – AI Impact Analysis

The 10 postings above reveal three distinct tiers in the 2026 QA market:

1. **AI-Native QA** (Jobs 1–4): Roles where the subject under test *is* an AI/LLM system. Require prompt engineering, RAG evaluation, and hallucination testing skills. Salaries: $150K–$200K+. These roles did not exist before 2023.

2. **AI-Augmented QA** (Jobs 5, 9): Traditional QA roles now listing GitHub Copilot, ChatGPT, or "AI understanding" as expected skills. Salaries: $1,000–$162K depending on market. This tier represents the majority of 2026 postings.

3. **Traditional QA** (Jobs 6–8, 10): Classical automation/manual roles with no explicit AI requirement. Still in demand but facing salary pressure at entry level as AI automates routine tasks.

**Conclusion:** AI is bifurcating the QA market — creating high-value AI specialist roles while compressing compensation for routine QA work. QA professionals who fail to develop AI tool proficiency by 2027 risk significant career displacement.
